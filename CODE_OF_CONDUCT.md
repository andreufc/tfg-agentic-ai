# Code of Conduct

## Project Nature

This repository contains the source code for **tfg-agentic-ai**, a Final Degree Thesis (*Treball de Fi de Grau*, TFG) submitted as part of the Bachelor's Degree in Data Science and Engineering (GCED) at ETSETB, Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC).

The project is the individual academic work of **Andreu** ([@andreufc](https://github.com/andreufc)), developed under the supervision of Prof. Sergi Bermejo.

## Academic Context

This is **not** an open-source community project. It is a solo academic work, and as such:

- **External contributions are not accepted.** The thesis must reflect the author's own work to comply with the academic requirements of the degree program.
- The repository is private until the thesis defense. It will be made public afterwards, for transparency and to allow reproducibility of the experimental results.
- All code, results, and writing are subject to the academic integrity standards of the degree program. Third-party code, datasets, libraries, and ideas are cited and attributed appropriately throughout the project.

## Supervision

The thesis is developed under the guidance and revision of the academic supervisor. Feedback and corrections from the supervisor are incorporated throughout the development of the project.

## Contact

For questions about the thesis, the methodology, or the code, contact the author via the email address listed on the GitHub profile. Once the repository is public, GitHub Issues are also welcome.
